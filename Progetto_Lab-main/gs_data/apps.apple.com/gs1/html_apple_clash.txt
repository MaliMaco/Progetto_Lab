I’m not sure that I have ever written a game review but I am hoping Supercell’s developers hear enough reviews like this to actually make a change. It is all but impossible to overcome a deck with all level 13 when you have a mix of 10 and 11. It’s gotten to the point that I usually just quit as soon as it becomes obvious that the person I am playing against isn’t terrible because I just have nearly 0% chance to win otherwise. Not only do they have better cards typically, I will go and check the player profile afterwards and they often have many more trophies than I do. You know a matchmaking system is broken when you are consistently outgunned and compete against players with higher achievements/skill in the game. I really can’t make sense of the matchmaking algorithm. I am sure they put a ton of work into it but it is about as bad as it gets. This is only in reference to war. Single player matchmaking is actually pretty good. As background, I actually play 6 accounts ranging from 4300 trophies on my lowest to a high of 5800 on my best account. The only reason I even play war is to get points for my clan and because I don’t want to let them down. I HATE having to play war and definitely wouldn’t if I wasn’t in a clan.
