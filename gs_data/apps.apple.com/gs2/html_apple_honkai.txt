                <div class="review-text">This game may have its issues with powercreep, but I'm pretty sure it will stay playable for f2p, seeing as a large portion of the player base prefers saving money, or simply keeping the challenge. I'm hoping that isn't compromised anytime soon. Regardless, HSR is my favorite gacha game I've played (I played the other 2 big dogs in the gacha world.) The quests are always either funny, profound, engaging, or comforting. The events are very entertaining and well-thought out, but I must ask you to provide more of them! After finishing an event, I feel dread because I don't want to run out of them so quickly, and it seems that Hoyo is a bit slow on pumping them out. The characters are very much my favorite aspect of the game, with lots of nuance, different motivations, and they're not always fixated on the MC, thus letting each character have its own independent value, with whatever unique role they play. The combat is great, I love being strategic but not being stressed by real-time combat, as my hands and fingers hurt sometimes and don't allow me to react quickly. The game environments are engaging, and I hope Hoyo can put out as many puzzles / chests out as possible, as it's one of the easiest ways for f2p to quickly and mindlessly collect stellar jades, though it does take time to find them. Overall, HSR is so relaxing to play, but interesting and "personal" in a way too. The dialogue is pretty good!</div>

I’ve also seen complaints about too much story and not enough gameplay. I don’t disagree with any of these points. HOWEVER, my personal experience with this game in the month since I’ve started playing has made it clear that you can’t please everyone and there are bound to be issues. This is made even more apparent considering how much FUN I’ve had with this game. Between the main story, the development of specific characters and introductions of new ones, the music, the graphics and sound design as well as the combat visuals, I can honestly say I’ve never had a more pleasant experience playing any mobile game. There were times where the story stagnated a little, but getting involved in the events and learning new styles of playing made up for that. And I ABSOLUTELY HATED the boss fight with Phantylia most of all. But I never spent a penny and I eventually strategized well enough to scrape through it despite the challenges. This is all purely objective and long-winded. But I believe this is an excellent game overall and I don’t regret picking it up.
